Identification Brief

Mark "Oz" Geist

Marine Corps veteran (1984–1996). Deputy Sheriff and small-town Police Chief in rural Colorado. Private security contractor in Iraq and Libya. Member of the Annex Security Team in Benghazi on September 11–12, 2012 — the night that became "13 Hours."

Co-author of the bestselling book that put names and faces to a story Washington tried to tell without them. Founder of the Shadow Warriors Project, advocating for contractors carrying invisible wounds. And now — host.
